# Simple Interest Calculator

A simple project and guide for calculating simple interest based on principal amount, interest rate, and time period.

## Formula

$$I = P \cdot r \cdot t$$

Where:
- **I** — Simple Interest
- **P** — Principal amount (initial investment or loan)
- **r** — Annual interest rate (as a decimal, e.g., 5% = 0.05)
- **t** — Time period in years

## Total Amount

$$A = P + I = P(1 + r \cdot t)$$

## Example

If **P = $1,000**, **r = 5% (0.05)**, and **t = 2 years**:
- **Interest (I):** $1,000 × 0.05 × 2 = $100
- **Total Amount (A):** $1,000 + $100 = $1,100# simple-interest-calculator
